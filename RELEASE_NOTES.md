## GLB&ME 1.0.1

### Fixed
- **The tool palette covered the whole screen** in the editor. It's now the slim column on the right edge as intended, so the model is visible while you work.

### New
- **Hide interface** – tap the eye button in the top bar to hide all panels, tools, the gizmo and the selection outline and look at your model unobstructed. Tap it again to bring everything back.

Install over 1.0.0 – your projects are kept.
