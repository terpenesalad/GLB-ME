## GLB&ME 1.0.0 – first release

Create, sculpt, paint, animate and export 3D models on Android. Every feature is included – no subscriptions, no account, works offline.

**Install:** download `GLB-ME-1.0.0.apk` below and open it on your phone (allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser/file manager if asked). Requires Android 8.0+ and OpenGL ES 3.0.

### Highlights
- **Sculpting** – Clay, Draw, Inflate, Smooth, Flatten, Pinch, Crease, Move, Drag, Layer, Mask and Paint brushes with X/Y/Z symmetry, falloff curves and stylus pressure.
- **Modeling** – 11 editable primitives, 3D text, image planes, transform gizmo with snapping, face edit mode (extrude, inset, delete, separate, flip), subdivision, decimation, smoothing, symmetrize, UV projection.
- **20 templates** – characters, props, nature, game assets and dioramas, several animated.
- **Materials** – full glTF PBR (metal/rough, emission, opacity, double-sided, unlit), 17 presets, 5 texture slots, vertex painting.
- **Rendering** – PBR with soft shadows, 10 matcaps, environments, wireframe, screenshots with transparent background.
- **Animation** – keyframe timeline, auto-key, turntable generator; exported as glTF animation.
- **Import** GLB · glTF · OBJ · STL · PLY – **Export** GLB · glTF · OBJ · STL · PLY, or share straight to other apps.
- Projects autosave as GLB files with your editor state.
